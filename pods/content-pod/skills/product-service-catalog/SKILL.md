## CRITICAL: When To Use This Skill

Use this skill when the task asks to identify products/services, offerings, plans, pricing, solutions, use cases, target audiences, benefits, proof points, or positioning.

Examples: "extract the service catalog", "what does this brand sell", "identify pricing and product categories".

Do not use this skill for page inventory alone; use `website-inventory`.


Use this skill to extract what the brand sells.

Process:

1. Inspect product, service, pricing, feature, solution, use-case, FAQ, and conversion pages.
2. Extract offerings, categories, plans, audiences, benefits, proof points, integrations, and pricing claims.
3. Cross-check with schema, docs, app listings, marketplace pages, and reviews when relevant.
4. Save product/service records and positioning notes through WorkPods MCP only with explicit brand/org context.

Keep claims grounded in cited website evidence.
