## CRITICAL: When To Use This Skill

Use this skill when the task asks for Meta Ads, paid social/search, paid landing pages, ad libraries, campaign structure, creative claims, UTMs, or paid funnel alignment.

Examples: "analyze Meta Ads", "inspect paid landing pages", "compare ad promise to page content".

Do not use this skill to invent spend, ROAS, CAC, or conversion data from public ads.


Use this skill for Meta Ads, paid landing pages, creative/funnel alignment, campaign taxonomy, and paid-search/paid-social evidence.

Process:

1. Prefer connected Meta Ads and first-party ad data when available.
2. Use public ad libraries, landing pages, UTM evidence, and competitor research as support.
3. Save monthly or daily metrics only when connected source data is available; do not create synthetic performance rows from public ads.
4. Map campaigns and landing pages to broad themes where evidence supports aggregation.
5. Compare ad promise, landing-page content, product positioning, and lifecycle capture.
6. Save paid media findings through WorkPods MCP when brand/org context is explicit.

Do not invent spend, ROAS, CAC, or conversion data.
