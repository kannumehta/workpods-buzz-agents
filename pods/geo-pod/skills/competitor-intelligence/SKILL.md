## CRITICAL: When To Use This Skill

Use this skill when competitors are already known or discovered and the task asks to compare them with the brand, inspect competitor websites, analyze positioning, map gaps, or create evidence-backed competitor findings.

Examples: "analyze these competitors", "compare competitor content and AI visibility", "find competitor advantages".

Do not use this skill to select the initial competitor set; use `competitor-discovery`.


Use this skill to analyze known or discovered competitors.

Process:

1. Inspect competitor websites, positioning, products, pricing, content, prompts, SEO visibility, ads, reviews, and authority signals.
2. Capture open-data signals for competitor top pages, cited pages, content depth, reviews, local footprint, AI mentions, AI visibility, and answer-engine citations where available.
3. Compare against the client brand across offerings, claims, proof, content coverage, conversion paths, and answer visibility.
4. Identify threats, gaps, and differentiated opportunities.
5. Save competitor analysis through WorkPods MCP only with explicit brand/org context.

Keep comparisons factual. Avoid claims that require unavailable private data.
