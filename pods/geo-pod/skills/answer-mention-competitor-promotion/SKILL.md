## CRITICAL: When To Use This Skill

Use this skill when the task asks why competitors appear above the brand, why the brand is absent from AI answers, how competitors are framed, or what content/proof/citation gaps could improve answer-engine inclusion.

Examples: "analyze competitor promotion in answers", "why does ChatGPT recommend competitor X", "find gaps causing AI answers to skip us".

Do not use this skill for competitor discovery; use `competitor-discovery`. Do not use it for raw scan execution; use `answer-engine-scan-execution`.


Use this skill to analyze when answer engines promote competitors over the client brand.

Process:

1. For each prompt, compare brand mention presence, order, explanation quality, citations, and competitor framing.
2. Identify why competitors are favored: stronger category pages, third-party mentions, clearer schema, reviews, authority, pricing clarity, or topical coverage.
3. Recommend fixes that can plausibly change answer behavior.
4. Save promotion analysis and action links through WorkPods MCP when brand/org context is explicit.

Do not claim causality beyond the evidence.
